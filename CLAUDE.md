## Perfume project memory
The user is building a perfume concept from landscape photos (horses, mountains, rivers).
When they send one of those photos or ask "where is this place?", read `perfume/PLACES.md`
and answer from it in Arabic: the place name and location, a full profile of the place, its
mood (fear, relaxation, calm, joy, tranquility) and the fragrance idea.
